# Research: predatory mobile game mechanics, and their non-predatory "baby versions"

Compiled overnight for MergeBoy. It covers how the games behind the fake ads actually make money,
what players complain about, and what MergeBoy does instead.

**Sources:** App Store, Google Play and Reddit pages mostly block automated fetching. Player
sentiment here comes from:

- Review aggregators: grand-screen, justuseapp, game-solver, marlvel, kimola.
- Regulator rulings: UK ASA, US FTC.
- Lawsuits.
- Trade press: PocketGamer.biz, Game Developer, Naavik, mobilegamer.biz.

Weak or AI-aggregated sources are flagged inline. Player opinions are paraphrased, with links to
the original wording.

**Could not be verified:**

- An ASA ruling against Hero Wars.
- A fake-ad complaint about Count Masters specifically.
- Outcomes of the State of Survival class action and the Coin Master gambling suit.
- The Monopoly GO "$25k teen" story, which traces back to a deleted Reddit post.
- Travel Town sentiment, which comes only from an AI aggregator.

> **Status in MergeBoy** is marked like this: ✅ built, 🟡 partly built, 💡 proposed.

## Part 1: Game by game

### Merge army / 4X "fake ad" strategy

**1. Top War: Battle Game** (Topwar Studio / Rivergame). 🟡 *Last Army: Merge War*

- **Loop:** merge identical units and buildings into higher tiers, then auto-battle and play a 4X world map with alliances.
- **Pressure points:**
  - Build and research timers with speed-ups.
  - Troop capacity limits.
  - VIP and alliance competition.
  - Pop-up packs. ([Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/en/insights/top-war-may-end-up-being-a-top-player-in-4x-strategy-games/))
- **Complaints:**
  - Apptopia's review analysis ranks false advertising as the top complaint.
  - Players say the ads show battles that don't exist.
  - Players call the game a time-and-money sink. ([grand-screen](https://grand-screen.com/games/top-war-battle-game/reviews/))
- **Fake-ad gap:** the ads show real-time fights or a car smashing things while soldiers merge. The UK ASA investigated two TikTok ads, which TikTok then pulled. ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/rivergame-ltd-g22-1166092-rivergame-ltd.html))
- **Baby version:** a merge grid of soldiers, then auto-battle against a visible enemy power.
- **Instead of paying:** the next enemy's power is always shown, so there's no surprise wall. Running out of food or energy sends you to another game.

**2. Last War: Survival** (FUNFLY). ✅ *Crowd Run 3D* + *Last Army*

- **Loop:** a "shoot the numbered gates" runner sits on top of a big 4X base builder.
- **Pressure points:**
  - VIP levels 1–18, plus paid VIP time that expires.
  - $1–3 starter packs that add extra build queues.
  - Speed-ups.
  - Alliance and server-war pressure.
  - Power creep. ([FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/how-last-war-survival-raked-in-16-billion-in-a-mere-18-months/), [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/last-war-survival))
- **Fake-ad gap:** the ads (including ones with Antony Starr) show a squad-growing shooter that barely exists in the game. A YouTuber built the ad's game as a real app. ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.com/Jemand-hat-tatsaechlich-eines-dieser-Fake-Werbespiele-gemacht-und-es-ist-ueberraschend-gut.1188721.0.html), [economics of fake ads](https://lancaric.substack.com/p/the-real-economics-behind-fake-ads))
- **Baby version:** gate runner → squad → boss. **Crowd Run 3D** does this, and survivors join Last Army.

**3. Kingshot** (Century Games). 💡

- **Loop:** medieval 4X with heroes, alliances and PvP. ([review](https://www.tonyreviewsthings.com/kingshot-ios-review-2025/))
- **Complaints:**
  - Players installed it expecting the tower-defense game from the ads.
  - Reviewers call it pay-to-win. ([grand-screen](https://grand-screen.com/games/kingshot/reviews/))
  - Ads with footballers drew criticism as misleading. ([Insider Sport](https://insidersport.com/2026/06/23/harry-maguire-misleading-kingshot-ads/))
- **Baby version:** a tiny tower-defense lane: 4 archers on 8 tiles against 3 waves.

### Base building with timers

**4. Whiteout Survival** (Century Games). 💡 *"Keep Warm" furnace game*

- **Loop:** a frozen 4X game built around a Furnace that heats the town.
- **Complaints:**
  - Reviews call it fully pay-to-win, with every event built around spending. ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6443575749/whiteout-survival/reviews))
  - A player petition asks for slower update rollouts. ([Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/stop-unbalanced-updates-restore-phased-rollouts-in-whiteout-survival))
- **Baby version:** keep a furnace fed with coal (from the Grinder) through telegraphed blizzards.

**5. State of Survival** (FunPlus / KingsGroup). 💡 *parody fake-discount shop*

- **Fake reference pricing:** a 2022 class action alleges bundles showed "original" prices that never existed (for example, $9.99 "down from" $997.40), plus fake "Only 1 remaining!" labels. I found no ruling. ([Pollock Cohen](https://www.pollockcohen.com/state-of-survival), [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/79925/funplus-and-kingsgroup-face-class-action-lawsuit-for-misleading-prices/))
- **MergeBoy's Starter Pack already admits its fake "was" price.** Proposed next: a minesweeper-lite "Supply Run".

**6. Evony: The King's Return** (Top Games). ✅ *every ad game is playable from the home screen*

- **The ASA upheld a complaint:** the ads showed puzzles, but the core game is city-building, and falling behind there locks you out of the puzzles. ([ASA ruling](https://asa.org.uk/rulings/top-games-inc-a23-1213924-top-games-inc.html))
- **MergeBoy twist:** the puzzle in the ad is the puzzle on the phone, and it's never gated.

**7. Rise of Kingdoms** (Lilith). 🟡 *alliance help = your interns*

- **Pressure:** VIP buffs, whale-dominated events, and free players being told to support spenders. ([RoK Guides](https://riseofkingdomsguides.com/is-rise-of-kingdoms-pay-to-win-game-pay-to-win-vs-free-to-play/))
- **MergeBoy twist:** "alliance help" in Clash of Kingdoms comes from your own interns. Nothing is competitive.

**8. Clash of Clans** (Supercell). ✅ *Clash of Kingdoms*

- **Pressure:**
  - Gem skips on a nonlinear price curve.
  - Scarce builders.
  - A 2014 breakdown describes the model as monetizing impatience. ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/clash-of-clans-time-monetization-formulas-demistifyed))
- **MergeBoy twist:**
  - No gems.
  - Every hand-played action in any game takes time off construction ("hustle").
  - Errands can finish builds outright.
  - The second builder comes from the Town Hall, not a $19.99 pack.

### Match 3

**9. Candy Crush Saga** (King). ✅ *Match Royale*

- **Pressure:** 5 lives on roughly 30-minute refills, extra moves, boosters, and begging friends for lives. ([Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/candy-crush-you-play-youre-hooked-now-what/))
- **Lawsuit:** a 2014 Chicago suit alleged lives were deleted to drive sales. That's an allegation, and I found no resolution. ([NBC Chicago](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/candy-crush-lost-lives/53870/?amp=1))
- **MergeBoy twist:**
  - Lives refill in 10 minutes, or right away through an errand in another game.
  - Losing never takes back what you matched.

**10. Royal Match / Royal Kingdom** (Dream Games). ✅ *Match Royale's "SO CLOSE!" screen*

- **"+5 moves":** an analytics vendor claims levels are tuned so players often fail by 1–2 moves. ([FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/games-redefined-match3-with-flawless-execution-and-smart-ua/), vendor claim)
- **Escalating continue prices:** one reviewer saw a continue's coin cost more than double after a second fail. The source page now returns 404.
- **Other pressure:** limited-time bundles, and events that feel rich in rewards followed by offers that imitate that abundance. ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/royal-match-analysis))
- **MergeBoy twist:**
  - The "+5 moves" paywall pauses your level.
  - You run an errand in another game, then come back to the same board.
  - The price never escalates.

**11. Homescapes / Gardenscapes** (Playrix). ✅ *Rescue the King (pull the pin)* + 💡 *renovation*

- **The ASA banned two pin-pulling ads in 2020:** the games are mostly match-3 plus renovation, and the pin levels are rare. ([Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/popular-mobile-games-gardenscapes-and-homescapes-called-out-for-misrepresentation-of-game-in-ads))
- **MergeBoy twist:** the pin game is a whole app. ⭐ Stars come from match-3 and pay for Clash of Kingdoms buildings.

### Pull the pin

**12. Hero Wars** (Nexters). ✅ *Rescue the King*

- **Complaints:**
  - Players never find the ad puzzles.
  - Progress is slow without paying. ([Trustpilot](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/www.hero-wars.com?page=10), [Alucare](https://www.alucare.fr/en/les-avis-hero-wars/))
  - A fan guide says the pin game is a minor side feature. ([h-w.fun](https://h-w.fun/en/hero-wars-is-different-from-ads/?amp=1))
- **MergeBoy twist:** 9 hand-made, verified physics levels. Replay any of them.

### Screw puzzles, idle games, merge-2

**13. Screw Jam and other nuts-and-bolts clones** (Rollic and others). ✅ *Screw Loose 3D*

- **Pressure:**
  - Interstitials after every level.
  - Rewarded ads required for coins.
  - "+1 hole" boosters.
  - Lives.
  - Difficulty walls around levels 100–240.
  - "Remove ads" purchases that reportedly don't remove all ads. ([game-solver](https://game-solver.com/screw-jam/))
- **MergeBoy twist:** no real ads, every level is solvable, and plates drop scrap for the whole factory.

**14. Idle / incremental tycoons**. ✅ *Grind the Thing* + interns

- **Pressure:** hidden offline-earnings caps, "watch an ad for 2×", and "remove ads" that doesn't. ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1435697033/rocket-star-idle-tycoon-game/contact))
- **Egg, Inc.** is the transparent counterexample. ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/egg-inc-idle-games-gold-standard/))
- **MergeBoy twist:** the offline cap (8h) is stated openly. The 2× boost is a joke ad you can always choose to watch.

**15. Merge-2 energy games** (Merge Mansion, Travel Town, Gossip Harbor). 🟡 *Merge Boy*

- **Pressure:** energy is the wall. Metacore says openly that the out-of-energy moment became a top ad placement. ([Metacore](https://metacoregames.com/news/introducing-ad-monetisation-without-compromising-player-experience/))
- **Proposed:** give Merge Boy an energy bar, refilled by errands.

### Dice and spin energy

**16. Coin Master** (Moon Active). ✅ *Goose Master*

- **Pressure:**
  - 5 free spins an hour.
  - Spin bundles from $1.99 to $99.99.
  - A double-down bet.
  - Raids that play on loss aversion.
- PocketGamer.biz called it "a slot machine masquerading as a game" ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-coin-master-monetise/)).
- Parent guides object to the slot mechanics. ([Elternguide](https://www.elternguide.online/en/popular-game-apps-coin-master/))
- **MergeBoy twist:**
  - Spins never regenerate and can't be bought. Each win in another game earns one.
  - Odds are printed on the machine.
  - No double-down.
  - Raids only hit an NPC goose, who leaves apology notes.

**17. Monopoly GO!** (Scopely). 💡 *Board Lap*

- **Pressure:**
  - Dice refills that can't keep up with daily goals.
  - Roll multipliers up to ×1000.
  - Sticker albums with rare gold stickers.
  - Partner events that need friends. ([IGGM](https://www.iggm.com/news/monopoly-go-dice-shortages-sticker-grinding-and-pay-to-win-mechanics-are-driving-players-crazy))
- **Proposed:** dice come only from wins elsewhere, multiplier capped at ×3, and duplicate stickers trade in to guarantee completion.

### Gacha

**18. RAID: Shadow Legends** (Plarium). 🟡 *Lucky Crate* + 💡 *Capsule Hero*

- **The Norwegian Consumer Council's "Insert Coin" report** uses it as a case study for gacha, layered currencies and manipulative offers. ([NCC PDF](https://storage02.forbrukerradet.no/media/2022/05/2022-05-31-insert-coin-publish.pdf))
- **Precedent:** in 2025 the FTC fined HoYoverse $20M over Genshin loot boxes. ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/genshin-impact-maker-fined-20m-over-deceptive-loot-boxes-and-child-privacy-violation))
- **Proposed:** odds and a pity counter printed on the button, useful duplicates, and no paid pulls.

### Survivor-likes / hypercasual

**19. Survivor.io** (Habby). 💡 *Survivor-lite*

- **Pressure:** energy per run, upgrade costs, and late-chapter walls. ([marlvel, AI summary](https://marlvel.ai/apps/survivor-io/reviews))
- **Proposed:** 3-minute runs with no energy. When you hit a wall, it tells you which game to play for the upgrade.

**20. Count Masters / Mob Control** (crowd-gate runners). ✅ *Crowd Run 3D*

- **Pressure:** interstitials after nearly every level, and "remove ads" purchases that disappoint. ([chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/id1562817072))
- **MergeBoy twist:** no energy bar and no ads. Losing offers a "revive" errand.
